# Style With Charm — WordPress Theme

> Minimalist, elegant, Pinterest-inspired WordPress theme for home decor publications, interior designers, and lifestyle creators.

Official Website: [stylewithcharm.com](https://www.stylewithcharm.com/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Pinterest-Inspired Masonry Grid**: Dynamic Pinterest-style feed for the latest inspiration posts, auto-updating as new WordPress posts are published.
- **Dynamic Category Exploration**: Automatically queries all WordPress categories and renders responsive cards with image covers.
- **Editor's Picks Magazine Section**: Featured 2-column layout highlighting sticky or curated home decor articles.
- **Progressive AJAX "Load More"**: Smoothly loads older posts without page reloads.
- **Warm Editorial Typography**:
  - Headings: *Cormorant Garamond* (Google Serif font)
  - Body & UI: *Jost* (Clean modern sans-serif)
  - Palette: Soft Ivory, Warm Cream, Soft Beige, Light Taupe, and Charcoal.
- **Editorial Single Post Layout**:
  - Breadcrumbs (Home / Category / Post Title)
  - Pinterest Pin button (direct image & title sharing), Facebook, and Email sharing
  - Dynamic "You May Also Like" related articles grid.
- **WordPress Customizer Integration**: Real-time management of hero banner, headline, subtitle, newsletter copy, and social links in Appearance > Customize.
- **Dedicated Page Templates**:
  - page-about.php (About Us editorial story)
  - page-contact.php (Minimalist contact form)
  - category.php & rchive.php (Category archives)
  - search.php (Fullscreen search modal + search results page)

---

## 🚀 Installation

1. In WordPress Admin, navigate to **Appearance** > **Themes**.
2. Click **Add New Theme**, then **Upload Theme**.
3. Upload style-with-charm-theme.zip and click **Install Now**.
4. Click **Activate**.
5. Customize settings under **Appearance** > **Customize**.
